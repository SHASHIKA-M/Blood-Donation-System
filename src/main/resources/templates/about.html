About BloodDonation.lk

Bridging the gap between blood donors and recipients across Sri Lanka to ensure no patient goes without life-saving blood.

Who We Are

Dedicated to Saving Lives in Sri Lanka

Are you in need of blood or looking to make a life-saving donation in Sri Lanka? Our Blood Donation Finder platform is here to connect voluntary donors directly with patients, coordinators, and certified hospital blood banks.

Blood cannot be manufactured synthetically; it only comes from generous voluntary donors. Through intuitive digital tools, real-time availability tracking, and emergency alert broadcasting, our platform eliminates critical delays when time matters most.
