End-To-End Process

From Your Arm To A Patient

Step 01

Register & Health Check

Sign up in two minutes, then a quick pulse, blood-pressure and haemoglobin screening confirms you are ready.

Step 02

Book & Donate

Pick a slot at your nearest blood bank and relax — a safe whole-blood draw takes only 8 to 10 minutes.

Step 03

Lab Screening

Every unit is tested for HIV, Hepatitis B & C, syphilis and malaria by QA officers before release.

Step 04

Match & Deliver

Hospitals request compatible stock; cold-chain couriers deliver it to the patient's bedside. Lives saved.

Eligibility Criteria

Can You Donate Blood?

To protect both donor health and recipient safety, the National Blood Transfusion Service defines clear medical eligibility guidelines.

Age 18 – 60 Years

First-time donors should be under 55 years old. Regular donors may donate up to age 65 upon clinical evaluation.

Weight Above 50 kg

Donors must weigh at least 50 kg (110 lbs) to safely donate standard whole blood units (350ml or 450ml).

Every 4 Months

A minimum of 4 months (120 days) must have passed since your previous whole blood donation.

Hemoglobin ≥ 12.5 g/dl

A quick finger-prick test is performed at every donation center to verify healthy iron levels.
